validatePOST .../validate with evidence sets validated: true. Required before production.
rollbackPOST /v1/models/{id}/rollback returns production to previous_production.
Who may do whatregister, validate, transition: operator or admin. Into production, or out of it: admin only.
Denials are auditedEach refusal writes a deny entry with a reason: not_validated, not_approver, policy_denied, not_found. A transition to production or a rollback is also evaluated by the active policy under stage.production; no active policy denies.
Dataset linkA registration can name a dataset_version. It must exist in the DatasetRegistry, otherwise 404.
Side effects of every change (register, validate, transition)
HMAC-chained audit entry
Lineage node, edge to the previous one
